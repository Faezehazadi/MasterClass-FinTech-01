# Session 1

## 1. Quick reviews

The three notebooks build the core Python data-analysis workflow:

1. `01_numpy_quick_review.ipynb` — arrays, statistics, vectorized arithmetic, and Boolean indexing.
2. `02_numpy_matplotlib_quick_review.ipynb` — category comparisons, fruit measurements, distributions, and time-series plots.
3. `03_numpy_matplotlib_pandas_quick_review.ipynb` — DataFrame inspection, filtering, missing values, price-per-square-metre calculations, grouping, and city summaries.

## 2. California cities

`california_cities.ipynb` analyzes 482 California cities. It prepares area and elevation measurements, creates a population map, ranks the ten most populous cities, compares population with city area, and calculates population density.

## 3. Billionaires

`billionaires_analysis.ipynb` cleans the Forbes 2021 billionaire dataset, converts the `NetWorth` field into numeric billions, and visualizes the highest net worths and the most common wealth sources, industries, and countries.

## 4. Lionel Messi goals

`lionel_messi_goals_analysis.ipynb` explores 790 recorded club goals. It analyzes goals by season, competition, match-minute bucket, goal type, and venue after preparing dates, minutes, and home/away indicators.

## 5. Online retail metrics

`online_retail.ipynb` works with the Online Retail transaction dataset. It calculates revenue and monthly growth, then investigates UK monthly active customers, order volume, average revenue per order, new-customer ratio, monthly retention, and cohort retention.
